Privacy policy

Findings definition review · Last updated November 16, 2026

This site lets invited reviewers read definitions of imaging findings and leave notes on them. It is a small research tool, not a commercial service.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your email address and the name on your Google account. We ask Google for nothing else; we cannot see your contacts, files, mail or calendar.
  • What you enter: the display name you choose, your notes and replies, and which findings you mark as reviewed, with the time of each.

Anyone can read the definitions without signing in; nothing is collected from visitors who do not sign in.

How it is used

Your email address is used only to check whether you are on the reviewer list. Your display name is shown beside your notes to other reviewers. Notes and review marks are used to improve the definitions. We do not sell or share this information, use it for advertising, or use it for any other purpose.

Who can see it

Only signed-in reviewers on the list can see notes, display names and review marks. The project administrators can see the reviewer list of email addresses. The data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database, and the page itself is served by GitHub Pages.

Keeping and deleting your data

Notes are kept for the duration of the review project. You can edit or delete your own notes at any time on the site. To have your account and everything you wrote removed, ask the project owner, or open an issue at github.com/stan-hua/definition_reviewer/issues.
